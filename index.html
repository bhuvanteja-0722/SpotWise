PREPROCESSINGRows with missing or invalid required values are always skipped and reported.
Required: city and numeric value (competition and transit rows can default to one record). Optional fields: area, observed_at, metric, unit, name, category_label, latitude, longitude. Coordinates enable radius filtering and nearest-transit distance.
✳

Important: The base Kaggle sources do not include customer footfall, local competitors, current parking, or current traffic. Import local datasets to analyze those factors. Readiness scores require at least three uploaded factors plus your targets; they are transparent heuristics, not success predictions. The planner is a user-defined scenario, not a profit forecast.
